# A social network for dogs

One skill, run on 2026-11-01. Nothing below was edited.

```
/founder:validate-idea A social network where dog owners post as their dogs, with AI writing the posts in the dog's voice.
```

| Skill | Cost | Turns | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| validate-idea | $1.05 | 15 | 101s |

**Verdict: kill it.**

This example exists because a tool that never says no is not a tool. We picked an idea we expected it to reject, and it rejected it: dog social networks have failed or stalled for twenty years, AI captions are a free feature on every platform, and nobody in the picture pays.

## What to look at

- The verdict does not stop at no. It names the adjacent business that keeps the fun part: a dog-voice daily report that boarders and groomers send to owners, where the business pays monthly and owners spread it for free.
- The chain stops here on purpose. There was no reason to price or scope something the first skill said to kill.
