# Go-to-market: office coffee subscription in Baku

Assumption: no launch date was given. This plan uses 4 weeks of pre-launch (Nov 2 to Nov 29, 2026) and a launch on Monday, Dec 1, 2026.
Assumption: the offer follows the pivot in `validate-idea.md`: white-label beans from a Baku roaster plus machine service, about 670 AZN/month for a 50-person office (Estimate).
Assumption: primary buyer is Leyla (office manager, 40 to 150 people), launch channel is Elvin (coworking founder), per `persona-gen.md`.
Assumption: 1 USD ≈ 1.70 AZN (Estimate).

## 1. Launch readiness

**Is the MVP enough?** Not yet. No experiment from `validate-idea.md` has run. The minimum to launch:
1. A signed white-label bean supply (one Baku roaster, fixed price per kg).
2. One technician on call with a written 24-hour response promise.
3. A one-page contract with one monthly invoice.
4. A WhatsApp Business number and a shared sheet of client machines, stock and visits.

No app, no roastery, no website beyond one page.

**Enough users?** Estimate: 40 offices per business center you can walk into × 15 centers ≈ 600 doors. You need about 20 paying offices for a 13,400 AZN/month business (20 × 670). At a 3% door-to-paid rate, 600 doors gets you 18. It's tight but it works, and coworking spaces add more leads.

**Biggest risk:** a slow repair in the first 60 days. One office down for 3 days with 70 people complaining kills the referral that every later sale depends on. Mitigation: keep one spare loaner machine before you sign client 5.

## 3. Launch day (Monday, Dec 1, 2026)

Of the five standard launch platforms, only LinkedIn fits. Product Hunt, Hacker News and X reach global tech audiences, not Baku office managers. r/azerbaijan exists (78,761 subscribers per a [third-party mirror](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/subreddits/azerbaijan), unverified on reddit.com) but is mostly news and diaspora discussion, and a local B2B service pitch would likely be removed as self-promotion. The three platforms that fit:

**LinkedIn**
- Post from your personal profile, not a company page (Estimate: a personal profile usually reaches more people than a new company page).
- Format: a short story, no link in the post body. "On Monday we delivered our first beans to [Coworking partner]. Here's what 4 weeks of visiting office kitchens taught me." 3 numbers from your survey, one photo of a real delivery, the WhatsApp number in the first comment.
- Post Tuesday Dec 2, 9:00 to 10:00 Baku time (Estimate: weekday mornings work best for B2B). Tag the coworking partner and any trial office that agreed.
- Reply to every comment within 2 hours. DM every office manager or HR person who reacts.

**Instagram**
- Launch Reel (the 45-second video) plus a Story series from the coworking tasting day.
- Collab post with the coworking space so it shows on both accounts.
- Link in bio goes straight to WhatsApp, not to the website.

**In person: launch tasting day**
- Run a free tasting bar in the lobby or kitchen of your coworking partner from 9:00 to 12:00.
- Bring a sign-up sheet with 3 fields: company, headcount, machine model. Every company that signs gets a call within 48 hours.
- Same week, run one more tasting in a Baku White City or Port Baku office that agreed to host.

2. **Business center management** at Baku White City or Port Baku: get listed as the recommended tenant coffee service in their welcome pack.

**Growth tactic for this product:** the "next floor" credit. After each install, give the office manager a card: "Know an office manager in this building? If they sign, you get a free month." One business center has dozens of offices, and your delivery route is already there, so each new office in the same building costs you almost nothing to serve.

Calendar note: corporate New Year parties in late December are a reason to offer a free tasting bar in every prospect office. Rashad-type buyers set budgets in December, so send one proposal to 3 large companies even though you won't close them this quarter.

| Printed one-pagers, machine stickers, referral cards | 80 | Local print shop |

| Reserve | 100 | Coffee for one community event |

**Free:** LinkedIn posts, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business, door-to-door, coworking partnerships.
**Worth paying for:** samples and tasting days. Tasting is the moment people say yes.

**Best spend under $200:** a free machine deep-clean for 10 offices, about 300 AZN (≈ $175) for technician time and descaling supplies (Estimate). It gets you through the door with no price talk, and it shows the office manager the problem you solve: the scale and dirt that come out of their machine. Every clean ends with the trial offer.
