# Idea validation: cozy mobile puzzle game with a playground meta layer

Assumption: "playground meta layer" means a space the player builds or decorates with rewards earned from puzzles, similar to the room decoration meta in match-3 games.
Assumption: the business model is free-to-play with ads and in-app purchases, because that is the default on mobile. If you plan a premium price, Q1 and Q4 change.
Assumption: nothing is known about your background, art skills, or past launches.

## The 30-second assessment

This is a solution looking for a market slot, not a problem. "Cozy puzzle + decorate a space" is the most crowded format on mobile, owned by studios that spend $100M+ a month on ads. A solo developer with no ad budget, on mobile, free-to-play, is fighting on the one front where you are guaranteed to lose. The game can be good and still get 150 installs.

## The five fatal questions

**Q1: Who is the customer, and would they pay today?**
The player is a woman aged 25 to 45 who plays 10 to 20 minutes on her phone in the evening to unwind, already has Royal Match or a sort puzzle installed, and watches cozy game clips on TikTok. She pays today, but she pays the game she already has. Most free-to-play players never pay at all. Estimate: if 2% of players spend $10 a month, that is about $0.20 per monthly active user, before Apple's 30% cut. Willingness to pay for *your* game specifically: maybe. That's a no.

**Q2: Why hasn't someone built this already?**
Everyone has.
- [Royal Match](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/royal-match/) by Dream Games: puzzle plus a light decoration meta, [past $3bn in user spending](https://mobilegamer.biz/dream-games-royal-match-has-passed-3bn-says-appfigures/), and it won by becoming [the biggest ad buyer in mobile gaming](https://www.gamigion.com/dream-games-monopoly-one-match-3-game-became-the-biggest-ad-buyer-in-mobile-gaming/). Naavik notes it [is hard to replicate](https://naavik.co/digest/why-dream-games-success-is-a-challenge-to-replicate/) even for funded studios.
- [Dreamy Room](https://felixbraberg.substack.com/p/we-played-dreamy-room-its-brilliant-50d): a cozy tidying puzzle, claimed $27M+ a year from ads (podcast claim, not verified).
- [A Little to the Left](https://gamedeveloper.com/press-release/a-little-to-the-left-cleans-up-with-999-999-sales) (small studio, about 1M copies) and [Unpacking](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2022/11/cosy-indie-darling-unpacking-has-sold-over-1-million-copies-since-launch) (1M copies in a year): the small-team cozy puzzle wins happened **on PC and Switch, sold at a fixed price, with a publisher**, not on mobile free-to-play.
- The long tail: thousands of cozy sort puzzles like [Cozy Animal Sort](https://appgoblin.info/apps/6756031004), estimated under $10K a month with tiny install counts.

What's different now: nothing in your favor. Puzzle CPI is about [$3 on iOS and $2 on Android](https://segwise.ai/blog/cpi-ipm-roas-benchmarks-optimizing-ad-spend/) (2024), and a 2026 guide puts casual and puzzle at [$3 to $5](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-03-17-user-acquisition-cpi-benchmarks-2026/). On Steam, "cozy" is hot but one analysis calls small cozy puzzle games [the most contested, least rewarding corner of the genre](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/cozy-games-steam-2026-niche-report).

**Q3: What's the distribution advantage?**
None stated. Mobile store search for "puzzle" is owned by apps with millions of reviews, and featuring by Apple is a lottery ticket, not a plan. The only free channel that works for cozy games is short video: satisfying 10-second clips on TikTok and Shorts. That only works if the core mechanic is visually unique. A playground meta is not clip-worthy on its own; the puzzle moment has to be. There is no built-in growth loop: a single-player decorate meta does not invite friends. Unless players can share or visit each other's playgrounds, every user is acquired by hand.

**Q4: Can this be a big business, or is it a feature?**
The playground is a feature. Every incumbent already has a meta layer and can reskin theirs in a sprint. Ceiling is not the issue (the category is billions); your share is.
To reach $1M a year:
- Free-to-play, Estimate: at $0.05 to $0.10 average revenue per daily user, you need about 27,000 to 55,000 daily players ($1M / 365 / $0.10 = 27,400). With 30-day retention around 5 to 10% for casual games (Estimate), you need hundreds of thousands of installs a year. At $0 budget, that requires a viral clip every month.
- Premium, Estimate: at $4.99 minus a 30% store cut, about $3.50 each, you need about 285,000 sales a year. A Little to the Left did roughly 1M over about 15 months, with a publisher.

**Q5: Can the founder actually build this?**
Unknown, and it matters. The hard parts are not code:
1. **Level design at volume.** Free-to-play puzzle games need hundreds of levels plus a live content cadence. Solo, that is the job that never ends.
2. **Art.** Cozy lives or dies on art and feel. If you are not a strong artist, this is a "first hire" problem before launch.
3. **Monetization and live ops.** Economy balancing, ad mediation, events. Studios run teams for this.

## Validation experiments

**1. Is the core mechanic clip-worthy?**
- Test: a 10-second clip of the puzzle moment gets organic reach.
- How: mock 5 different puzzle mechanics as short animations (Figma, After Effects, or a 1-day throwaway prototype recorded on screen). Post one per day on a fresh TikTok and YouTube Shorts account with hashtags #cozygames #puzzlegame. Post nothing about the meta layer.
- Success: at least one clip passes 50,000 views, or 3 of 5 pass 10,000. Below 2,000 each means the mechanic is not your distribution.
- Time and cost: 10 days, $0.

**2. Does the playground pull people in, or is it noise?**
- Test: the meta layer increases interest compared with the puzzle alone.
- How: make two key art images and a one-line pitch. A: puzzle only. B: puzzle plus playground. Post each as a separate concept post in r/CozyGamers and 2 cozy gaming Discords, a week apart, each linking to a Tally form email signup. Track click-to-signup per variant.
- Success: B gets at least 1.5x the signup rate of A, and 200 total signups. If A wins, cut the meta.
- Time and cost: 2 weeks, $0.

**3. Does PC/Switch-style premium beat mobile free-to-play for you?**
- Test: people will commit to your game on a store where discovery is free.
- How: pay the $100 Steam Direct fee, publish a "Coming soon" page with the best clip from experiment 1 and the winning art from experiment 2. Share it in the same communities and in replies to your top clips.
- Success: 500 wishlists in 14 days. Steam wishlists are free intent you cannot get on the App Store before launch.
- Time and cost: 2 weeks, $100.

## The honest verdict

**Pivot it.** The game idea is fine. The channel is wrong. Go **premium, PC and Switch first, mobile port later** (or pitch Apple Arcade or Netflix Games once you have a vertical slice and wishlists). That is where small cozy puzzle teams have actually won, because wishlists, festivals like Wholesome Direct and Steam Next Fest, and publishers replace the ad budget you don't have. Build a mechanic you can show in 10 seconds, and make the playground shareable (export a picture, visit a friend's) so it becomes a loop instead of a menu.

The single most important thing this week: run experiment 1. If no mechanic clip gets traction, no amount of meta layer will save the game.
