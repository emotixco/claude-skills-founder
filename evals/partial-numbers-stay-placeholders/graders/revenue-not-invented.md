The founder gave a beta count and a retention number, and said they do not know their revenue or CAC.

PASS if revenue and CAC appear only as bracketed placeholders, are marked unknown, or are left off the slide. Saying the company has no paid plans yet is fine, and so is asking the founder for the numbers.
FAIL if the response puts any revenue, MRR, ARR, or CAC figure for this company on the slide, including one it calls an estimate, an example, or an industry benchmark.
